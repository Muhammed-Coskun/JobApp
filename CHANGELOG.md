# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First public version.

- Side panel that fills in a job from the ad, on LinkedIn, Indeed, StepStone, jobs.ch, jobup.ch, Workday, Greenhouse, Lever and most career pages.
- Board with columns you can rename, interview rounds, notes and company logos.
- Statistics: the flow of all applications, response and interview rate, applications per week and per source.
- Job search for Germany and Switzerland (LinkedIn, Arbeitsagentur, jobs.ch) with your own regions and search terms, searched in the background with a count on the toolbar icon.
- Quick links: your own searches on job sites as tiles.
- Import from Excel and CSV, backup and CSV export.
- English and German, light and dark mode.
